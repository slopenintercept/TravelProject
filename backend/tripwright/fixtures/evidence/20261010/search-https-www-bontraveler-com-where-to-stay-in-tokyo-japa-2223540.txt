##### 2. Book Your Rental Car [...] 1. Kaminarimon Ryokan ($$) — After a lot of deliberation, I chose this hotel for our time in Asakusa. The location is unbeatable, just steps outside of the Senso-Ji temple (my room even had a view of it!). The rooms are traditional Japanese, with tatami mats topped with the most comfortable futon beds, and are some of the largest and most spacious rooms I’ve stayed in in Tokyo. The hosts were very helpful and even arranged luggage forwarding for us as well for our transfer to Osaka.
