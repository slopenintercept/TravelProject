## Kiyomizu-dera FAQ

### How much is Kiyomizu-dera admission in 2026?

Admission is ¥500 for adults and ¥200 for elementary and junior-high students.

### Do I need to reserve Kiyomizu-dera in advance?

No timed reservation is normally required for an ordinary individual visit. Buy the standard ticket at the entrance.

### Standard Temple Admission

Buying the standard ticket directly at the entrance is sufficient. There is no strong reason to use a third-party platform for admission alone.

 Adults: ¥500
 Elementary and junior-high students: ¥200
 Ordinary individual visitors do not normally need advance reservations
 No group discount is offered
 Visitors with an original qualifying disability certificate and one companion may enter free
