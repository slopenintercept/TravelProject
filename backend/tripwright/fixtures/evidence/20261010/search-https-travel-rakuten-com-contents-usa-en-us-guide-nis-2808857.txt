The market is open daily, though hours vary by shop. Most vendors are open between 10:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m. Weekday mornings are best for fewer crowds and friendly conversations with shopkeepers, while weekends bring more energy and samples. Many stores close on Wednesdays, Sundays, and public holidays, so it’s worth checking ahead if you’re visiting with a specific stall in mind.
