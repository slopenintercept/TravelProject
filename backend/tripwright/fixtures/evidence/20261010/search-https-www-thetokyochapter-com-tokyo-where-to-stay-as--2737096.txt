9. Hatchobori  
Hatchobori is an affordable place to stay in Central Tokyo. Most people have to change trains when travelling out to Tokyo Disneyland anyway so this is a nice in between,   
The area is quite so it’s great for families with kids who get overstimulated easily.   
– 2 mins on the train to Tsukiji Market– Easy access to Team Lab Planets, Odaiba and Kidzania  
-Walking distance to the Tokyo Station area, Ginza and NihonbashiI have written in detail about the Hatchobori area here.
