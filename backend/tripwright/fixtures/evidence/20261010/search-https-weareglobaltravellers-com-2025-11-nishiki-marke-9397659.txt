📍 Address: Nishikikoji-dori, Nakagyo Ward, Kyoto  
Opening Hours: Typically 9:00am–6:00pm (varies by stall)  
Entry Fee: Free

## A Little History & Fun Facts About Nishiki Market

Before Nishiki Market became the bustling foodie favourite that it is today, Nishiki Market started as a simple fish market more than 400 years ago [...] Go early or mid-morning to beat the crowds. By lunchtime, it’s packed.
 Bring cash as many stalls don’t accept cards.
 Don’t rush. Leave time for wandering, as the best part of Nishiki is sampling as you go.

### Best Time to Visit Nishiki Market

Nishiki Market is open year-round, but there’s a different kind of buzz in the spring and autumn when Kyoto is overflowing with seasonal produce. Think sakura-themed sweets in spring and chestnut or pumpkin flavours in autumn. [...] Visiting and wandering Nishiki Market really is a must-do experience that captures the city’s traditional heart. Nishiki Market is one of the best things to do in Kyoto because of the sensory immersion of smells, colours, and flavours that it delivers.
