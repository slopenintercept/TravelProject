The most popular times to visit Kyoto are for cherry blossom season in early April and fall colors season in mid to late November. From a beauty perspective, these are the “best” times of year in Kyoto. It’s world-renowned during both seasons, and these are the peak travel seasons for good reason. [...] Another thing to keep in mind when planning is that the peaks for both sakura and fall colors seasons do not have fixed dates. During an abnormally warm spring, we’ve experienced the actual peak of cherry blossom season in late March when no crowds were around. We’ve also been in mid-November during what should have been the heart of autumn foliage season only to find little change in color. I’ve been to Kyoto for over 50 ‘tourist nights’ and have over 110 temple stamps in my Goshuin (book for collecting temple stamps) but I won’t return because of the crowds.
