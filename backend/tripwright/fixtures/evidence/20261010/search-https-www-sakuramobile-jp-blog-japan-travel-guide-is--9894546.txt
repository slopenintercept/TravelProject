 Tokyo Haneda International Airport [...] ### Vehicles in Japan

Due to strict taxi regulations in Japan, Uber didn't enter the market until 2018 and only expanded into Tokyo in 2020 through agreements with established taxi companies.  
  
This slow entry was met with opposition from local taxi drivers, who cited concerns over undercut fares and passenger safety. Because of the restrictions, Uber's availability often depends on the area and the time you need a ride.

### Rural Areas and Alternative Transportation [...] The quick answer to this question is yes. Uber is available in Japan.  
  
However, there are regulatory limits to Uber, and it functions differently compared to other parts of the world.  
  
In Japan, Uber acts as middleman to hail a licensed taxi for you, and it is generally considered one of the more expensive transportation options in the country.
