Located at the foot of the “Storm Mountains” in the northwest of the city of Kyoto, this world-famous, seemingly endless forest of swaying bamboo boughs is perfect for a relaxing retreat to nature during a stay in Japan.

The Arashiyama bamboo grove is open 24/7 and admission is free. The surrounding Arashiyama district, known for unique fishing methods which involve using trained cormorant birds, is also well worth a visit. [...] The sound of the rustling bamboo within this forest has been named one of the “100 Soundscapes of Japan” by the Japanese Ministry of Environment. Together with Fushimi Inari and the Kinkaku-ji, it offers a Kyoto experience not to be missed.

The bamboo grove is open 24 hours a day and boasts no entrance fee, so during peak season, it is recommended that you visit before 9 am or in the evening.

## How to get to Arashiyama Bamboo Forest From Kyoto Station [...] Admission is ¥800 for adults aged 16 and over and ¥400 for children aged 4 to 15; children aged 3 and under enter free. Entry hours are 9 am to 4 pm, with the summit open until 4:30 pm. The park is closed on January 1 and may also close on irregular dates, in bad weather, or earlier if the monkeys return to the mountain.

## Kameyama-kōen Park

At the end of the bamboo forest, you can get away from the crowds and reach the magnificent views of Kameyama-koen.
