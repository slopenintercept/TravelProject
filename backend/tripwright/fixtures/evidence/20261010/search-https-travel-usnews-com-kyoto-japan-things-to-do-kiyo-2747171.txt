## Tickets

For ordinary visiting hours, admission is 500 yen for adults (about $3.50) and 200 yen (about $1.50) for students. Ask about the special tour given in English for international visitors.

To reach the temple, take bus No. 100 or 206 from Kyoto Station to Gojo-zaka or Kiyomizu-michi bus stops, which are both a 10-minute walk from the temple.

How we rank things to do [...] ## Hours

Generally, the temple opens at 6 a.m. and closes at 6 p.m. daily. However, during the summer months (July and August) and special evening illuminations, the temple may have extended hours, closing at 6:30 p.m. or 9:30 p.m., respectively. The dates change annually; an up-to-date timetable is available on Kiyomizu-dera Temple's website.
