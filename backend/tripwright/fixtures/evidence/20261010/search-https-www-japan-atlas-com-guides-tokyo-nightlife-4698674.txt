## Shibuya After Dark

Shibuya is Tokyo’s most viscerally exciting nightlife district, anchored by the famous scramble crossing that becomes a river of humanity after 9pm. The area splits into several distinct zones once you know where to look. [...] Each of Tokyo’s nightlife districts has a distinct personality, and picking the right one matters more than picking the right bar. Golden Gai suits travelers who want an intimate, conversation-driven evening and don’t mind small spaces. Shibuya and Roppongi suit larger groups who want energy, music, and later closing times. Shimokitazawa suits anyone tired of tourist-facing spots and craving something more local.
