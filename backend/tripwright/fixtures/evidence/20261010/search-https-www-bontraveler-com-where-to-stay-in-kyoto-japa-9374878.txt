### Short on Time? Here Are My Top Picks for Where to Stay in Kyoto:

For first-time visitors, the best places to stay in Kyoto are Downtown and Southern Higashiyama. Both will put you closest to the best things to do. If you’re staying 4+ nights, I’d consider splitting your time between both or adding in a special stay in Arashiyama. [...] Fushimi: While alluring as Fushimi is as its home to the temple, it’s very disconnected from the rest of the city.
 Budget accommodations without rail access: There are some serious cheap stays on the outskirts of Kyoto, but you’ll pay for it with transportation. Always pick something central with good access.
