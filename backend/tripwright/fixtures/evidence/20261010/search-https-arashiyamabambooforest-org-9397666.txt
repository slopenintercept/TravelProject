Quick answer

The Arashiyama Bamboo Forest is a public path through a mōsō bamboo grove in the Ukyo Ward of western Kyoto, open 24 hours a day with no entrance fee and no ticket of any kind. Walking the main path end to end takes 10–15 minutes, which surprises most first-time visitors, so plan the grove as one stop inside an Arashiyama morning rather than the whole outing. Before 8:30 the path is quiet; by 10 it is shoulder to shoulder.

Key takeaways [...] There is no entrance fee. There is no ticket, no booth and no fence: the path is a public right of way that happens to run through bamboo, so it is open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. A small charge existed before 2015 and has since gone. Anyone selling you an Arashiyama Bamboo Forest ticket is selling you a guided tour, a rickshaw ride or a seasonal night event, never admission to the grove itself.
