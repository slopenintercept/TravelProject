[...] There are around 10,000 cherry blossom trees throughout Sakurayama Park (Gunma Prefecture), 3,000 of which are Yoshinos that bloom in spring. However, the majority of them are Fuyu Zakura, which you can see in mid-October and November. That's right, April isn't your only chance to see cherry blossoms! Fuyu Zakura flowers are sparser than their springtime cousins, but they make a gorgeous contrast against the fiery autumn colors. [...] Kiyomizu Temple has long been one of the most celebrated temples in Japan. With beautiful views over the city throughout the year, from early to mid-April, cherry blossoms decorate the temple grounds and the city of Kyoto. During this time, the temple also holds a nightly illumination with spotlights shining on the pink trees, a beautiful sight for all guests.
