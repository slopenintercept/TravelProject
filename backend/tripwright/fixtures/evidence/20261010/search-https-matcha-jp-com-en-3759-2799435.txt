### Golden Dragon Dance and White Heron Dance

Sensoji Golden Dragon Dance

Twice a year on March 18 and October 18 the temple hosts a spectacular dragon dance known as Kinryu no Mai, during which a traditional golden dragon is paraded in front of the main hall. [...] Another incredible traditional parade called Shirasagi No Mai (White Heron Dance) is also held at the temple on the 2nd Sunday of April and November 3 every year.

### Hozuki Market

Hozuki Market

Taking place each summer from July 9 until 10, a popular Hozuki Market is set up at Sensoji, selling auspicious Hozuki lantern plants.
