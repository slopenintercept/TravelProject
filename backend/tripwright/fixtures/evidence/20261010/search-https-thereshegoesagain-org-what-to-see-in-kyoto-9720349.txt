### 9. Shoren-in Temple

Also known as Atawa Palace, we were told this temple from the 13th century was a bit more off-the-beaten-path. It’s pretty hidden and not as large as other temples or shrines nearby. [...] If you’re like me and you love to find that one spot that overlooks the whole city, you’ll love Kiyomizu-Dera Temple.

When you get off and start walking, you’ll see the bright orange entrance from far off. You can then walk in a little circle around the gardens. Apparently, there’s a stone called Tainai Meguri that will grant your wishes, but we couldn’t find it!
