Fushimi Inari is the most popular shrine in Japan, it receives approximately 10 million visitors each year. The best time to visit Fushimi Inari in order to avoid the crowds is either during sunrise, or at night time after 6pm. During normal hours, you will need to be mentally prepared for large crowds, especially during the peak tourism season, which occurs from mid-March to early April for the cherry blossoms, and at the end of November for the autumn leaves. [...] Here are the 3 best strategies for avoiding the crowds at Fushimi Inari:

1. Arrive at sunrise, which is around 7am during winter, and around 5am during summer. Arriving so early may be difficult, but the shrine has a completely different feeling when it's quiet and peaceful, it's well worth it.
2. Head deeper into the shrine, beyond the main viewpoint.
3. Go there at night time while everyone else is eating dinner.
