Quick answer

The Arashiyama Bamboo Forest has no opening hours and no entrance fee: the path is public and open around the clock. Go before 8:30 in the morning for a quiet path, or in the last hour of daylight once the coach groups leave. By 10:00 it is shoulder to shoulder, and it stays that way until late afternoon. Tuesday to Thursday are the quietest days of the week. The paid sights nearby open at 8:30 to 9:00 and close between 16:00 and 17:00.
