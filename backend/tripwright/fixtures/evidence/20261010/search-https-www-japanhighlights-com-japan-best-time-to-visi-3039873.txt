March to May is the best season to visit Japan for cherry blossoms. This is when the country looks its most beautiful. In most regions, cherry blossoms reach full bloom from late March to early April (around March 24 to April 2), making spring and Easter holidays an ideal time to travel.

If you want to avoid peak crowds and high prices, consider traveling a little earlier or later.
