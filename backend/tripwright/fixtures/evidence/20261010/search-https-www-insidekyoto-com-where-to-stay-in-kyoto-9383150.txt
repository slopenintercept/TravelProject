Where To Stay In Kyoto 2026
https://www.insidekyoto.com/where-to-stay-in-kyoto

First-time visitors → Downtown Kyoto
 Traditional atmosphere → Southern Higashiyama (Gion area)
 Easy transport/day trips → Kyoto Station Area
 Quiet, local feel → Northern Higashiyama
 Nature and escape → Arashiyama (best for a night or two)
