Trains, coaches, buses and taxis are plentiful and often get you where you want to go with little fuss.

Japan is much larger than it looks on the map and trains are usually faster than you would be permitted to drive.

One other thing that has deterred me from renting a car apart from Hokkaido and Yakushima is that if you have even a relatively minor accident you could be arrested and made to stay in Japan for the trial.
