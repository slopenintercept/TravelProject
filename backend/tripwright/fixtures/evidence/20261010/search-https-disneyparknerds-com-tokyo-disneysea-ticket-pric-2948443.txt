The Tokyo DisneySea ticket price in 2026 is variable by date, but for a standard 1-Day Passport you’ll usually see adults priced from 7,900 to 10,900 yen (about $51–$71 USD), juniors 6,600 to 9,000 yen (about $43–$59), and children 4,700 to 5,600 yen (about $31–$36). In practice, that means you can plan on “about 8,000–11,000 yen per person” for most days, then fine-tune your exact cost by picking your visit date on the official calendar when you buy.
