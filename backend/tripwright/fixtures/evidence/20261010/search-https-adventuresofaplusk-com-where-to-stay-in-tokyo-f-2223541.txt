#### Hotels

Tokyo is home to thousands of hotels. We found that while there were some international chains (like IHG, Marriott, etc), most hotels were either Japanese chains (APA Hotel, Super Hotel, etc) or one off hotel locations. In our opinion, if your budget can afford it, a hotel is the way to go! They can hold your bags, coordinate luggage transportation, and have other amenities to make your stay more comfortable.

#### Airbnbs [...] #### Airbnbs

You could also stay in an Airbnb if you want a more local experience or are traveling with a large group. Just keep in mind that not all Airbnbs can store your bags if you arrive or leave outside of check in/check out hours.

#### Hostels [...] Want to sleep next to Godzilla? Stay at Hotel Gracery Shinjuku! This hotel is located right in the heart of Shinjuku’s entertainment district, Kabukicho, inside the building where Godzilla breathes “fire” from. They have standard hotel rooms, but for the ultimate experience, stay on the highest floor (the Godzilla floor!). This entire floor and all of its rooms are Godzilla themed and you will have stunning city views from up here.
