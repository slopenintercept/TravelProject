Japan welcomes travelers in every season

The best time to visit Japan 2026 depends on your priorities. For cherry blossoms, plan for late March to mid April. For autumn foliage and pleasant weather, choose October to November. For fewer crowds and budget-friendly travel, consider winter months. A JR Pass can help you explore different regions to catch seasonal highlights across the country.
