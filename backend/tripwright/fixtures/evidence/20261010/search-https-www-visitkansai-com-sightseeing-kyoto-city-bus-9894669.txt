# Kyoto City Bus

The City bus is the most commonly used transportation in Kyoto. It’s convenient for the locals to commute and for tourist to visit almost all major temples and shrines in the city. With the One-day Pass which is only 500 yen, you can take buses as many times as you need in a day. Without the Pass, you pay 230 yen each time. [...] ## Major lines map

Balloons show the popular tourist sites. Blue zone is out of the flat fare area which requires additional 150 to 250 yen to the All Day Pass validity.

## Alternatives [...] People tend not to think about using taxis and trains when they have the One-day Pass. But a bus is not always the best way. When you have to wait the next bus for more than 15 minutes at a station, or on a day the traffic is busy for tourist season, consider the other transportation options. You may have heard that a taxi in Japan costs too much, but they are about 620 yen for the first 2km. Why don’t you share the ride with someone waiting for the same bus?
