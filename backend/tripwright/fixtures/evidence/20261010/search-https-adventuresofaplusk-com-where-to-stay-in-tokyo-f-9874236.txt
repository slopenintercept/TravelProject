Akasaka is considered to be one of the underrated places to stay in Tokyo for first time visitors. It may not be home to as many of Tokyo’s top attractions, but with easy access to multiple train stations, it is very convenient to explore multiple areas of the city. Plus you can find more budget friendly options! In fact, all of the hotels listed below often have nightly rates under $150 USD. [...] Since most first time visitors to Tokyo will visit here, it is often a top contender for places to stay. Not only because of what you can see just in Shibuya, but also because it’s really convenient to visit other areas of Tokyo, with Shibuya Station offering many train routes. However, we have found that the prices are a bit higher here, so if budget is a concern, we’d advise not staying here.
