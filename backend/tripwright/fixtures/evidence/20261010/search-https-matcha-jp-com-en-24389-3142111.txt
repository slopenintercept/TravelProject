### Opening Hours

Tokyo Disneyland typically operates from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM, providing guests with a full day of magical experiences. The park’s hours may vary depending on the season, special events, or holidays, so it's advisable to check the official website for the most accurate and up-to-date information regarding opening hours.
