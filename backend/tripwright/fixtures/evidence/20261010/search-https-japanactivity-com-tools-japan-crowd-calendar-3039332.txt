For many first-time routes through Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka, mid-May, October and early December offer useful balances of weather and demand. They are not universal winners: Hokkaido is strongest in other seasons, Okinawa has a subtropical climate, and Kyoto's autumn foliage can be as busy as blossom season. The best month depends on the destination and whether you value comfort, quiet, price or scenery most.

January–February

September–October

### From storm risk to mild days

September can stay hot and storm-prone. October is often comfortable, although event weekends and autumn travel build demand.

November–December

### Foliage, then quieter cities

November foliage is a peak in Kyoto and other scenic cities. Early December can offer value before year-end travel begins.
