## Meiji Jingu Entrance Fee 2026

#### Is Meiji Jingu Free?

Yes, Meiji Jingu is free. There is no admission fee for the shrine grounds, including the main hall, the torii gates, and the forest paths. Only a few areas inside the grounds charge a separate fee, listed below.
