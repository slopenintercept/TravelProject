### Short on Time? Here Are My Top Picks for Where to Stay in Kyoto:

For first-time visitors, the best places to stay in Kyoto are Downtown and Southern Higashiyama. Both will put you closest to the best things to do. If you’re staying 4+ nights, I’d consider splitting your time between both or adding in a special stay in Arashiyama.
