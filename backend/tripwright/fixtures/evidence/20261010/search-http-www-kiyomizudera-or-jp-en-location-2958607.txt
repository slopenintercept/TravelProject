# ACCESS GUIDE

## GETTING HERE

Map to Kyoto. From Osaka Itami airport: By airport, Limousine bus. from Osaka: By train, By airport limousine bus. From Tokyo: By shinkansen, By air from Haneda airport, By air from Narita airport. [...] \ Caution about online mapping apps:  
There are only two routes to the temple ground: one route from the Nio-mon Gate on top of the Kiyomizu-zaka hill and the other route from the emergency road entrance on the top pf the Chawan-zaka hill. Please be aware that some online mapping apps, such as Google Maps, may show you a route which doesn’t lead to the temple grounds.
