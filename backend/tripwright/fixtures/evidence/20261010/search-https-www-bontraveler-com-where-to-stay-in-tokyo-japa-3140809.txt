### Frequently Asked Questions: Where to Stay in Tokyo

#### What is the best area to stay in Tokyo for first-timers?

Shibuya is my number one pick for first-time visitors. The neighborhood has so much to do, great subway access for seeing the rest of the city, and good hotel options.
