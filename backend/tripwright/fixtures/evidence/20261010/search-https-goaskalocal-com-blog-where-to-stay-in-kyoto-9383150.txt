The Kyoto Station area is the best place to stay if you’re on a budget or will be using Kyoto as your home base for day trips around the region. It’s not super charming and it’s honestly a bit far from the main sights, but it’s got lots of very affordable hotels, public transport is good, and the convenience of being right next to the train station is unbeatable for day trippers.

 Matsubaya Ryokan - $160-200 USD
 Kyoto Tower Hotel - $100-150 USD

6. Northern Higashiyama (yellow) [...] I think that downtown Kyoto is the best all around area for first-time visitors. It’s central, well connected by transport, always lively, full of restaurants, and has lots of hotels at different price points. It’s not historic or atmospheric though, so won’t be right for visitors who want to wake up and be amongst the beautiful temples.

 Mitsui Garden Hotel Kyoto Sanjo Premier - $300 USD
 Kagihei - $150-170 USD

2. Gion (green) [...] It has strong neighborhood vibes and a very good dining scene, and it’s a great place to stay if you want a local atmosphere while still maintaining close proximity to the city’s big sights.
 Budget travelers and anyone looking for a good deal on a hotel should focus on the Kyoto Station area. Immediately surrounding the train station is a host of affordable hotels, including lots geared towards Japanese business people. Restaurants and shops are also correspondingly reasonably priced.
