For first time visitors to Kyoto, purchasing the Kyoto bus pass for the day is a no brainer at a cost of 600 yen per adult and 250 yen for children under 12. The pass is sold at the train station, the tourist information centre, or even at the hotel. If you intend on taking the bus twice at 230 yen each way, don’t get the day pass. [...] Kyoto City Bus will allow you to visit all the main attractions in Kyoto. For great savings buy a Kyoto City Bus Pass 1 day for 600 Yen, and you will get unlimited travel in 24 h. It will allow you to travel in Kyoto City Bus, Kyoto Bus and also Raku Bus (express bus for all the main tourist places).  
Buy them at Kyoto station Tourist Information Center close to Shinkansen tracks. Each journey ( irrespective of the distance costs 260 yen).So in case you plan to take three or more trips go for a one day pass which costs 600 yen.
