### HOW TO SAY THANK YOU [...] Here is the crowd prediction calendar for Universal Studios Japan.  
As with the Tokyo Disney Park crowd prediction calendars – choose the month and then look at the days.   The weather forecast is written under the day of the month and a letter “A-F”.   A is the least crowded and F is the most crowded.

  

The crowd prediction calendar for Universal Studios Japan. [...] Make sure you are looking at the correct month – 04= April , 05 =May etc.   
Green = least crowded  
Yellow = a little crowded  
Orange = crowded  
Red = very crowdedPredictions are based on high vs low season, weather, special events, days of the week, number of pre-purchased tickets so far, number of people who have annual passes etc.
