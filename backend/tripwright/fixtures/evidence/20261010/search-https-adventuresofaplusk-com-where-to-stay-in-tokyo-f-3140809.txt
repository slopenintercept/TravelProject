#### Hotel Mystays Premier Akasaka

While nothing fancy, Hotel Mystays Premier Akasaka is a good budget option in Akasaka. It’s located in a quiet area and offers the basics you need. Their twin rooms are also pretty spacious for a Tokyo hotel!
