I could imagine that there would be a sense of how the culture really lives in a host home, may be even get advice as to where to go. However, I could also see staying in a Hotel would be beneficial as you literally don't have to worry about anyone else, [...] in the same street and station in 3 minutes. Western beds (don't try the mats. Westerners can't live on the floor).   Looking only at value for your money : airbnb wins. Always. Specially when portable wifi is included. Look out for listing's that state "close to subway station" in big cities. Those are your main target. You don't want to walk 15 minutes from the station to your home.
