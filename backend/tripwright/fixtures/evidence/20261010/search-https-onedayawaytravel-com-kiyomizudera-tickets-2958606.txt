## Quick Answer: Kiyomizu-dera Admission Fee 2026

The Kiyomizu-dera admission fee in 2026 is ¥500 for adults and ¥200 for elementary and junior high school students. You do not need to book standard admission in advance. Tickets are sold at the gate on the day of your visit. [...] During these special periods, Kiyomizu-dera is scheduled to stay open until 9:30 PM, with last entry at 9:00 PM. The illuminated buildings, seasonal scenery, and the temple’s blue beam in the night sky create a very different atmosphere from a daytime visit.

If your trip overlaps with one of these dates, a night visit can be one of the most memorable things to do in Kyoto at night. Just expect crowds during peak cherry blossom and autumn foliage periods.
