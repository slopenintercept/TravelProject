## Best time to visit Kinkaku-ji Temple

While the Kinkaku-ji Temple opening hours are 09:00 am to 05:00 pm daily, note that the best time to visit is as close to opening as possible, especially during peak tourist season when the cherry blossoms are in bloom.

You will avoid the crowds that typically build up towards the mid-morning, and will have ample opportunity to snap photos from coveted spots along the garden path. [...] The entrance fee to Kinkakuji Temple is currently at 500 yen for the general public tickets and a discounted 300 yen for primary and middle school students.

In current exchange rates, this translates to roughly $3, a nominal charge for such a stunning attraction! [...] You will purchase the tickets at the entrance gate before entering the temple grounds. No advance booking or reservations are currently available, but lines to pay are usually never lengthy, even during peak tourist season.
