Note: The Main Hall closes at 5:00 PM sharp. After this time, you cannot purchase charms (Omamori) or receive Goshuin (temple stamps).

### Nakamise Shopping Street (Sensoji Market Hours)

This 250-meter street from Kaminarimon Gate to the temple features about 80 shops. [...] Around 8:30 AM, take a stroll down Nakamise Street. Before the shops open, you can view the “Asakusa Emaki”—beautiful mural paintings on the shop shutters depicting the history of Asakusa.
