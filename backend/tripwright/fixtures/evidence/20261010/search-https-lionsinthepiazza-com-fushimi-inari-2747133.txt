With ticketless 24-hour access, Fushimi Inari offers more flexibility than most of Kyoto’s sites. Visiting outside of standard business hours frees up the day for other monuments – and avoids the worst crowds. The question is when to arrive.

## Orientation

Fushimi Inari Kyoto Honden Main Shrine Wood Structure Painted White And Orange [...] From our hotel in Gion, a four-kilometer taxi ride to Fushimi Inari cost less than ¥2,000. Beginning at 5 a.m., Kyoto’s transportation network offers sparse service which gradually picks up for the morning rush hour. The Inari station on the JR Nara train line is across the street from the site’s edge. Across the canal, the Fushimi-Inari station on the Keihan subway is an easy four-minute walk.
