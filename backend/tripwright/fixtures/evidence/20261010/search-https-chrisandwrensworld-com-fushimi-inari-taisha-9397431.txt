### Avoiding the crowds at Fushimi Inari

It’s no surprise that one of Kyoto’s top attractions can get seriously busy. However there is a way to avoid the masses, it just involves a very early alarm clock.

Most tourists in Kyoto tend to visit Fushimi Inari Taisha around 8/9am and arrive in swarms right through the afternoon. It might seem extremely early, but arriving at 6am is the best time to visit if you want a more peaceful and serene experience. [...] Fushimi Inari is open 24 hours a day, meaning it never shuts! Whether you want to visit for sunrise or sunset, you can come whenever you like.

Visiting at any time of day also allows you to avoid the crowds by visiting first thing in the morning or in the evening, when most tourists are back in the city.
