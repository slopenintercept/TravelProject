What you can expect when visiting Fushimi Inari in Kyoto...  
  
Entrance is Free & Fushimi Inari is open 24/7  
  
When to Go?  
⛩ Early Morning (before 6-8 AM) or Late Evening/Night: This is hands-down the best way to avoid the massive crowds. We went at 6:30am and had a fabulous experience. You can go before sunrise or after sunset for a more quiet experience. The gates are illuminated at night.
