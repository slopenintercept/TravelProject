So during super-peak season, that one-way reserved seat between Tokyo and Kyoto costs ¥14,250 (for Hikari or Kodama services). Meanwhile, the same ticket costs just ¥13,650 during off-peak season.

If you’re just making one trip, the price changes don’t make too much of a difference. However, if you’re planning something like a multi-leg journey, probably don’t do it during super-peak season. Or stick to non-reserved seating, where these seasonal surcharges don’t apply.
