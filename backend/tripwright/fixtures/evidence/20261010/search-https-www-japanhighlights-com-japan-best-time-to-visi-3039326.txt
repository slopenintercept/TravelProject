Autumn is also festival season. Enjoy evening maple leaf illuminations, the Jidai Matsuri in Kyoto, and art festivals in Tokyo. Expect colorful parades, open-air performances, and a mix of theater, dance, and music from artists around the world.

Compared to spring, autumn is less crowded. You move at a slower pace, take better photos, and enjoy each stop without rushing. It's one of the most relaxing times to explore Japan.
