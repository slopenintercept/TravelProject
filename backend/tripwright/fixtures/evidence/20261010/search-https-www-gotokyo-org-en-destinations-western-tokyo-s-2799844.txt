# Shibuya - the epicenter of modern Japanese culture

## The frenetic and scrambling center of modern Japanese culture [...] Artistic shots of Shibuya's iconic crossing continue to feature on the pages of global fashion and travel magazines, and for good reason. The chaotic, yet miraculously organized crossing has become a symbol of Tokyo's dynamism, and represents Shibuya—Japan's trend-setting powerhouse dedicated to fashion and arts. Join the crowds as they diverge off into the many streets and alleyways to shop and hangout in hip cafes, bars, and restaurants.  From Narita Airport: one hour 55 minutes by Limousine Bus, or one hour 40 minutes by train to Shibuya Station.  
 From Shinjuku Station: 7 minutes on the JR Yamanote Line to Shibuya Station.   
 From Tokyo Station: 23 minutes on the JR Yamanote Line to Shibuya Station.
