| Calmer times | Weekday evenings. On weekends the first hour, 6 PM to 7 PM, is busiest. | [...] Arashiyama Bamboo Forest is open 24 hours, but it is not normally lit at night. The only time it glows is Arashiyama Tsukitouro, a lantern illumination that runs from 30 Sep to 8 Nov 2026, 6 PM to 9 PM.

Entry to the paid bamboo area costs about US$9.50 (¥1,500) on the day. In advance, it costs about US$12.65 (¥2,000). Children 12 and under are free.
