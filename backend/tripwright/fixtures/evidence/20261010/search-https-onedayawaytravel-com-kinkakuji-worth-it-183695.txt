### How much is Kinkakuji admission?

Regular admission is 500 JPY for adults and 300 JPY for elementary and junior high school students. Special openings or events may have different conditions, so check the latest official information before visiting.

### Do I need to book Kinkakuji tickets in advance? [...] Right at opening: Arriving around 9:00 AM usually gives you your best chance of lighter crowds and calmer reflections on the pond. Do not expect an empty temple, though — school groups often arrive early too. “Early” at Kinkakuji means fewer people, not few people.
 Late afternoon: Visiting after around 4:00 PM can also be pleasant, and the lowering western light warms up the gold. Keep the 5:00 PM closing time in mind.
