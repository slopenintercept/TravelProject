## Getting there and around

Kinkakuji can be accessed from Kyoto Station by direct Kyoto City Bus number 205 in about 40 minutes and for 230 yen. Alternatively, it can be faster and more reliable to take the Karasuma Subway Line to Kitaoji Station (15 minutes, 260 yen) and a taxi (10 minutes, about 1500 yen) or bus (10 minutes, 230 yen, bus numbers 204 or 205) from there to Kinkakuji.
