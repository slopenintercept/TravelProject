#### Where to get an IC card?

IC cards can be purchased at ticket machines and ticket counters of the issuing railway companies. The initial cost consists of a refundable deposit of 500 yen plus an initial amount (typically 1500 yen) to be charged onto the card. For a surcharge, Suica and Pasmo cards can also be purchased and sent to you before the start of your trip through Japan Experience. Last but not least, Iphone users can add Suica, Pasmo and Icoca cards to Apple Pay. [...] type can be up to five yen cheaper than the other.
