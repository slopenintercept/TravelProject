# Tokyo to Kyoto: The Fastest and Cheapest Ways to Travel in 2026

July 10th, 2026 | Carey Finn

![](https://cdn.cheapoguides.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/09/IMG_1869-copy-162x162.jpg) Written by [Carey Finn](https://tokyocheapo.com/author/francesmaeda/)

In a hurry? Tokyo to Kyoto in 2026:

* **Fastest:** Nozomi Shinkansen — ¥14,170 reserved, about 2 hr 15 min ([book a one-way ticket on Klook](https://affiliate.klook.com/redirect?aid=513&aff_adid=774887&k_site=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.klook.com%2Fen-AU%2Frails-32%2F1012-japan%2Fsearch%2F%3Fdate_range_count%3D90%26origin_position_name%3DTokyo%26origin_position%3Dfbf76020-13a1-41b8-a33a-ce13ccdbabd9%26destination_position_name%3DKyoto%26destination_position%3D7fc793b8-f3a5-4f25-8ee9-cd76e89520e5%26departure_date%3D2023-11-17%26passengers%3D%5B%5D%26spm%3DMobility_Aggregate.SearchBtn%26clickId%3D62f6f68e0c))
* **Cheapest:** Overnight highway bus — from ¥2,800, and you save a night’s accommodation
* **Best value:** Hikari non-reserved — ¥13,320, about 25 min slower than the Nozomi but ¥850 cheaper

There are plenty of ways to get from Tokyo to Kyoto in 2026, and the right one depends on whether you care most about speed or price. The bullet train (Shinkansen) is our pick — it runs city centre to city centre in about 2 hr 15 min. If you would rather save the cash, an overnight highway bus is the cheapest way to make the trip, with budget flights sitting somewhere in between. Below are all the options, with current fares and how to book each one.

## How to get from Tokyo to Kyoto

Kyoto is about 370 kilometers (225 miles) west of Tokyo. It’s part of the Kansai region, and thus actually much closer to [Osaka](https://japancheapo.com/locations/osaka/): the two cities are about 30 minutes apart on the train. Coming from Tokyo, you’ll reach [Kyoto](https://japancheapo.com/locations/kyoto/) before Osaka.

### Top choice: Shinkansen

The **fastest, easiest, and most convenient option**is the Shinkansen, especially if you have a [Japan Rail Pass](https://affiliate.klook.com/redirect?aid=513&aff_adid=617672&k_site=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.klook.com%2Factivity%2F1420-7-day-whole-japan-rail-pass-jr-pass%2F). **The cheapest option** is usually a highway bus, followed by flights — but these are both a bit time-consuming.
