## Frequently Asked Questions about Day Trips from Tokyo [...] Can I combine two of these day trips in one day? Kamakura and Enoshima pair naturally and are commonly done together, since the Enoden line connects them directly. Chichibu and Nagatoro also combine well on the Chichibu Railway. Trying to combine any of the more distant options (Nikko, Hakone, Mt Fuji area) with anything else in one day is not realistic given travel time.
