Shibuya Tokyo Guide: Crossing, Shopping & Nightlife (2026)
https://www.tokyotourism.org/shibuya-guide

Shibuya can be as cheap or as splashy as you make it. The crossing, Hachiko, Center Gai and window-shopping the towers cost nothing, so a day here can run almost free if you skip the paid extras. Add Shibuya Sky and a couple of meals and you are looking at a very reasonable day out by Tokyo standards. Here is a rough per-person guide in 2026 yen. [...] Current 2026 prices, straight from the operator: booked online in advance, an adult ticket is ¥2,700 for entry before 15:00 and ¥3,400 from 15:00 onward; at the counter on the day it is ¥3,000 and ¥3,700 respectively, subject to availability. Children under 5 enter free, and children’s tickets are sold only at the counter. The deck is open 10:00 to 22:30, with last admission at 21:20, and there is no time limit once you are inside — most people stay around 90 minutes. [...] | Item | Budget | Comfortable |
