Fushimi Inari-taisha Shrine is a must-visit iconic landmark in Kyoto, lauded for its spiritual ambiance and stunning red torii gates. Easily accessible via nearby train stations, this free attraction offers a rewarding spiritual walk, particularly if you venture higher to escape the crowds. Visitors suggest spending 2-3 hours here, with early mornings or late evenings recommended for a quieter experience.
