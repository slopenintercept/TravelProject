## Nishiki Market Hours

Nishiki Market is generally open from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. To avoid the crowds, consider visiting in the morning or early afternoon, especially on weekdays.

Keep in mind that some shops may close earlier, particularly those that sell perishable items. Being mindful of these hours allows you to make the most of your visit and enjoy a more leisurely shopping experience.
