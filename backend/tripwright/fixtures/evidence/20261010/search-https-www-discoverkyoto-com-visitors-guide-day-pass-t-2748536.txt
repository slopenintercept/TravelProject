### How to Use [...] The Hankyu Hanshin One-Day Pass gives you unlimited access to all Hankyu, Hanshin, and Kobe Kōsoku train lines, which includes lines that connect to the cities of Kyoto, Osaka, and Kobe. If you want to explore all of these cities in a short space of time, this pass is a good choice.

One-day pass: ¥1,600 (no children’s fare available)

### Where to Buy [...] This pass only covers the subway and can be used for unlimited rides on all Kyoto Subway lines for the duration of one day. You can also get special offers at some tourist spots if you present this ticket on the day of use.
