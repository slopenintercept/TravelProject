Recommended tickets and passes:  
💳 Suica Card — Prepaid, tap-and-go IC card. Works on almost all Tokyo trains, subways, and buses.  
🚇 Tokyo Subway Ticket — Unlimited rides on Tokyo Metro + Toei Subway. Great for 1–3 days of sightseeing.  
🚅 Japan Rail (JR) Pass — Unlimited JR trains across Japan—including Shinkansen, airport trains, buses, and ferries.

### What are the options for Tokyo Metro tickets? [...] The Suica Card is a rechargeable prepaid Tokyo transport card that offers a contactless way to travel on trains, subways, and buses. I grabbed one of these at Haneda Airport on arrival and I found it to be incredibly convenient—you just load it up with credit then tap in and out at the ticket gates at any train station in Tokyo. [...] With Suica, you’ll be paying for each train fare separately from pre-loaded credit. Suica Cards can be easily reloaded with credit at station ticket machines, convenience stores, or via mobile apps.

💳 You can order a Suica Card online to be collected at Haneda or Narita airports, or you can get a Welcome Suica on arrival at the airports or at major train stations. It’s by far the most flexible option for catching trains around Tokyo.
