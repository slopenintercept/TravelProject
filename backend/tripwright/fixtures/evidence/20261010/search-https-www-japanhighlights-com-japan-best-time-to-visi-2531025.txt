Winter (December to February) is the most budget-friendly time to visit Japan, except around Christmas and New Year. The weather is cold and dry, with daytime temperatures around 10°C (50°F), so dress warmly.

This is the season to travel slowly. Ski in Hokkaido without long lift lines. Wander through the Sapporo Snow Festival without being pushed by crowds. Or soak in an outdoor onsen in Hakone, steam rising as snow falls around you. [...] March to May is the best season to visit Japan for cherry blossoms. This is when the country looks its most beautiful. In most regions, cherry blossoms reach full bloom from late March to early April (around March 24 to April 2), making spring and Easter holidays an ideal time to travel.

If you want to avoid peak crowds and high prices, consider traveling a little earlier or later. [...] Also, avoid four major Japanese holidays: Golden Week in May, Obon in mid-August, Silver Week in mid-September, and the New Year holiday from late December to January 3rd. During these four periods, the Japanese may use these dates to go on vacation. Tourist attractions will be extremely crowded, the costs for food and accommodation are greatly higher, and traffic is often congested.
