> The museum usually opens at 9 a.m. and closes at 10:00 p.m. It is generally quietest during the first two and last two hours on weekdays, with numbers peaking at noon until just after lunch. On weekends, the spread is a little more even, with people getting there early, so weekdays are, in our opinion, best.
